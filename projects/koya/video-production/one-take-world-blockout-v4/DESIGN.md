# Koya world blockout design

Concept angle: a truthful architectural white model with enough material coding
to make every threshold and adjacent space readable at normal playback speed.

- Neutral Brisbane daylight, pale blue sky and restrained natural materials.
- Koya massing is distinct off-white concrete, warm timber and dark railings.
- Public spaces remain deliberately schematic and concept-only.
- The resident is a simple sage/black proxy; no attempt to fake final realism.
- Camera path and parallax are the focal element. No decorative edits or cuts.
