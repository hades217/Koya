# Koya 106 Clip B V6 - Five-second camera choreography

This is one continuous moving shot. The time rows are motion checkpoints, not cuts.

## Camera lock

- perspective: first-person property-viewing camera;
- height: 1.60 m throughout;
- lens: 30 mm full-frame equivalent throughout; no zoom;
- stabilization: controlled gimbal walk with subtle physical footfall, no floating drone motion;
- roll: 0 degrees;
- pitch: level, with less than 2 degrees of natural walking variation;
- focus: deep architectural focus; no rack focus;
- speed: purposeful, approximately 1.2-1.5 m/s during the main advance;
- character: none visible;
- camera never stops before the final frame.

## Continuous five-second path

## Forbidden camera behaviour

- no cut at 2.5s or 3.5s;
- no pan from a stationary tripod pretending to be a turn;
- no digital zoom or Ken Burns scaling;
- no easing to a full stop before the doorway;
- no camera passing through the MPR wall, west stop wall, door leaf, vanity, toilet or shower glass;
- no crossfade between kitchen and bathroom;
- no architecture replacement during the clockwise turn.

`B0 -> B1 -> B2 -> B3 -> B4 -> B5` is a single camera spline. B1-B4 are geometry checkpoints only and are deliberately not uploaded as Seedance reference images, because multi-reference R2V caused spatial blending in V5.
