## Forbidden events

- High-rise skyline, rooftop, pool deck or unrelated communal terrace.
- Changing from Level 1 to a higher elevation.
- Replacing the wraparound terrace with a small remote balcony.
- Closing or relocating the threshold during the move.
- Losing the Living connection at the endpoint.
- Static-image push-in, floating camera, cut, dissolve, morph or teleport.
- Ultra-wide stretching or physically implausible terrace width.
