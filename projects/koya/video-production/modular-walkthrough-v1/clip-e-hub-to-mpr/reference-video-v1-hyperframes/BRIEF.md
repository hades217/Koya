## Concept angle

A brisk buyer's-eye inspection: public-room landmarks establish orientation, door-jamb parallax proves the crossing, then a decisive turn shows the modest room width and flexible use.

## Visual direction

Neutral Brisbane daylight, warm restrained Koya-compatible materials, rectilinear 32 mm-equivalent perspective and first-person eye height. The local scene is topology evidence and Concept Design, not a developer-issued interior or dimension claim.
