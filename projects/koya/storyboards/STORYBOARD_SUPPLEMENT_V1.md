## Speed and camera logic

- Geographic dive: 5.8s; A→C accelerates, D brakes, E performs the rapid pitch-up, F settles on the official facade, and G acquires the resident.
- Facade, sign and entrance: 6.5–7.5s; camera looks at the building first, then acquires the resident without stopping.
- Foyer to lift call: 2.0–2.3s; camera may move slightly faster than the resident and look toward the lift.
- Lift open and physical entry: 2.0–2.5s.
- Door close, compressed travel and reopen: 2.0–2.5s.
- Exit, corridor and target door: 3.5–4.0s; resident becomes smaller while the camera looks ahead.
- Total target: approximately 22–25s to the apartment door.

## Accuracy boundaries

- `OFF-LOC-001`, `OFF-LOC-002` and `OFF-EXT-001` are official marketing visuals from Koya.
- The geographic map is suitable for storytelling but is not a cadastral or legal boundary plan. Any final pin/route animation must be checked against the address before release.
- Foyer, lift cabin and corridor remain `Concept Design / Artist Impression`; no developer common-area design has been supplied.
- The active facade in 00F/00G remains the official image. The resident is a separate locked walking layer, not an AI redraw of the building.
- The lift-entry contact sheet's first panel showed closed doors and is not an active `lift open` anchor. Only the extracted approved-direction panels listed above are active.
