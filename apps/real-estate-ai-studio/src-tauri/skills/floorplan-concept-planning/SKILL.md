# Floorplan concept planning

Translate an accepted floor plan into room-specific concept briefs.

- Preserve room adjacency, doors, windows, fixed services, orientation, and circulation visible in the plan.
- Label generated imagery as a concept unless the client has accepted it as a canonical render.
- Separate spatial constraints from style direction.
- Ask for missing architectural evidence rather than silently completing the building.
