# Estate Studio Brand Concepts

Status: concept review only - not connected to production UI.

## Identity idea

The Estate Studio mark combines three ideas:

- An architectural `E` made from an upright plane and two projecting floor plates.
- An open doorway/portal that suggests entering a governed project workspace.
- A four-point aperture shared with Property AI as an intelligence and readiness signal.

The product logo uses only the product-neutral palette. Koya's project colours do
not recolour the Estate Studio master brand.

### Colour revision v2 — rejected

Status: **rejected by the project owner on 1 September 2026**. The files remain
only as design history. Do not use Sunlit Teal, Botanical Signal, or any
green-plus-dark pairing in the product, logo, sidebar, buttons, or marketing.

The v1 mark was structurally sound but visually too dark because every major
plane used near-identical slate values. V2 separates the architectural planes
and gives the intelligence aperture a clear focal colour.

### Identity revision v4 — Aster line mark

The architectural `E` identity is rejected. V4 starts from the approved mascot
instead: a reduced line drawing of Aster's asymmetric head and chest aperture,
paired with a quiet rounded wordmark. This follows a mascot-first, deliberately
under-designed identity model without copying another company's character.

- `estate-studio-logo-v4.svg`: primary horizontal wordmark.
- `estate-studio-app-icon-v4.svg`: utility app icon; not fused into the master wordmark.
- `estate-studio-logo-system-v4.svg`: review board showing scale and application.

V4 is a concept review asset and is not connected to production UI.

#### V4.1 shape refinement

V4.1 keeps the approved mascot-first direction while reducing the box-like
quality of the first line mark. It uses a lighter stroke, a ribbed side module,
a shallower chest block, a softer baseline, and a slightly lighter wordmark.

- `estate-studio-logo-v4-1.svg`: refined primary wordmark.
- `estate-studio-app-icon-v4-1.svg`: refined utility icon with a simplified small-size face.
- `estate-studio-logo-refinement-v4-1.svg`: proportion and scale review board.

Logo rules:

- Use the symbol alone below 80px wide.
- Preserve clear space equal to the width of the four-point aperture.
- Use the two-colour mark on light neutral surfaces.
- Use a single white or single slate version where contrast requires it.
- Do not add glow, gradients, shadows, bevels or a luxury crest treatment.
- Do not combine the mark with a house roof, key, map pin or skyline.

## Property AI mascot

Working name: **Aster**.

Aster is a compact architectural companion based on a physical scale-model
maquette rather than a generic robot. Its asymmetric head recalls folded
architectural planes. The four-point chest aperture connects the character to
the Estate Studio symbol.

Assets:

- `property-ai-mascot-sheet-v1.png`: pose and expression system.
- `property-ai-avatar-v1.png`: transparent square bust for chat/avatar testing.

Mascot rules:

- Use the avatar only for Property AI identity, onboarding and empty states.
- Never place the mascot as a large background behind chat content.
- At 32-48px, use the bust/avatar rather than the full character.
- The aperture may indicate AI status using the product accent or semantic state.
- Do not add speech bubbles, hard hats, keys, houses or real-estate props.
- Avoid exaggerated animation, cartoon facial expressions and glossy toy materials.
