# Off-plan evidence audit

Use official drawings and client-approved assets as the factual source of truth.

- Classify every source as official, approved render, floorplan-grounded concept, style-only concept, or unknown.
- Report absent facts as unavailable. Never infer dimensions, views, finishes, amenities, availability, or pricing.
- Block topology-sensitive production until the relevant floor plan and room identity are accepted.
- Keep drafts, accepted assets, rendered outputs, and public releases as separate states.
