# Apartment 106 Spatial Audit

## Clockwise narrative from entry

1. The entry is at the sheet-upper-left and opens into a narrow hall running toward the main apartment.
2. Storage is drawn along the hall. Laundry is the first wet/service room beside the hall.
3. Bath, Ensuite, WIR and Bedroom 1 occupy the sheet-upper band from left/centre toward the far right.
4. Bedroom 2 occupies the sheet-lower-left corner and opens to the public circulation along its upper edge.
5. The compact MPR sits immediately sheet-right of Bedroom 2. Its sheet-upper/right boundary is curved and enclosed; the only drawn public opening is at the MPR's sheet-lower-right corner beside Dining and the terrace-side circulation.
6. A linear kitchen runs across the upper side of the public room, with exactly one island below it.
7. Dining is below the island, between the MPR and Living zones.
8. Living occupies the sheet-lower-right public zone. Its sofa faces the solid internal wall below Bedroom 1, where the TV/display is drawn.
9. The Terrace wraps the sheet-down and sheet-right exterior edges of the public zone and continues beside Bedroom 1.

## Camera implications

- A buyer-viewing path must not enter MPR through its curved upper/right enclosure or central right wall; it must use T05 at the lower-right corner.
- The camera must not place a second island or route around an invented island.
- The sofa/TV relationship is a circulation landmark and must not rotate between frames.
- A route to the Terrace must cross a real opening in T10 and show frame/track parallax.
- Independent beauty renders are not evidence that any topology edge exists.
