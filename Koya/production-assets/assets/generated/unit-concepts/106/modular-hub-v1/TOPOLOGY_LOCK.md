# Apartment 106 - Modular Topology Lock

Source: official Type 106 floor plan. The labels below are production interpretations of the drawing, not dimensioned construction documentation.

## Clockwise geometry from entry

1. Entry and Hall sit at the northwest end.
2. Laundry and main Bath are immediately east of Hall and remain separate adjacent rooms.
3. Bedroom 2 occupies the southwest corner and is entered through its northwest inward-hinged door.
4. MPR sits immediately east of Bedroom 2 and south of the kitchen circulation. Its only door is at its northeast corner.
5. The linear kitchen runs west-east along the north side of the public space. A single island sits south of that line.
6. Bedroom 1 occupies the northeast/east side and is entered from the kitchen at its southwest corner.
7. WIR sits west of Bedroom 1. Ensuite sits west of WIR. The only valid sequence is Bedroom 1 -> WIR -> Ensuite.
8. Dining sits south of the island, between MPR and Living.
9. Living occupies the southeast public zone.
10. Terrace wraps the south and east exterior of Living and continues past Bedroom 1's exterior edge.
11. Living's south and east exterior boundaries are grouped full-height glazing/sliding-door assemblies opening to the wrap Terrace, interrupted only by the structural columns, jambs and frames shown on the plan. They must not be rendered as long decorated solid walls.

## Modular edit rule

Each room branch travels forward from a locked Living/Dining hub and ends at the destination. The final edit returns to the hub using an editorial cut; the model is never asked to invent a reverse route.

## Automatic rejection

- Bedroom 2 door on the south, east or southeast side.
- MPR larger than Bedroom 2 or presented as a full third bedroom.
- Direct kitchen-to-Ensuite or Living-to-Ensuite doorway.
- WIR omitted between Bedroom 1 and Ensuite.
- Two islands, an L-shaped second kitchen, or a public-space office/study not supported by the plan.
- Terrace appearing only as a remote front balcony rather than wrapping Living's exterior edge.
- A long solid or decorated wall replacing Living's south or east wrap-Terrace glazing.
- Public space rendered with an ultra-wide tunnel effect.
