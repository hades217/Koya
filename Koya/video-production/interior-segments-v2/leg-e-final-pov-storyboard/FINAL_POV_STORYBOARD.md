# Apartment 106 — Final POV Walkthrough Storyboard

Status: superseded before submission. Seedance was not submitted. The active production plan is `Koya/video-production/MODULAR_LIVING_HUB_PRODUCTION_PLAN.md`.

## Sales objective

The continuation is a literal first-person property inspection. The camera represents the buyer. After the existing guide leaves the frame at the start, no person, hand, body, reflection, or performance appears again. Every beat must explain the apartment layout rather than stage a character moment.

## Floor-plan authority

- Apartment 106 official plan: `Koya/production-assets/assets/official/floorplans/apartment-106.png`
- Internal area shown on the plan: 99 sqm.
- Remaining route: MPR threshold / kitchen -> Bedroom 1 -> WIR -> Ensuite -> same route back through WIR and Bedroom 1 -> kitchen -> Dining -> Living -> Terrace.
- Bedroom 1 is entered from the north side of the kitchen.
- WIR and Ensuite are reached only from inside Bedroom 1.
- Dining is south of the kitchen; Living is southeast; Terrace wraps the exterior edge of Living.
- One kitchen island only. No added corridor, office, second island, or substitute doorway.

## 30-second route after the existing 56.50-second endpoint

The open-plan Living/Dining area is the orientation hub. It must be established before the private suite is inspected, and it must reappear after the suite return. This gives the buyer a stable spatial reference instead of making the camera immediately disappear into another room.

## Living-room depth lock

- Use a natural 32-35 mm full-frame perspective, not an 18-20 mm ultra-wide lens.
- Keep verticals level and the sofa / coffee table at normal residential scale.
- Dining is the first midground anchor after the island; Living sits beside it, not far down a corridor.
- The terrace opening is on Living's exterior edge. It must be crossed physically; it cannot dissolve into an outdoor image.
- Reject any frame that adds a study/office zone, stretches the room into a showroom, duplicates furniture, or places the terrace as a remote straight-ahead destination.

## Continuity locks

- One uninterrupted daytime take.
- No cuts, dissolves, wipes, crossfades, occlusion swaps, wall pass-throughs, or room morphs.
- No people after 1.5 seconds.
- Same warm oak joinery, pale stone, off-white walls, daylight direction, camera height, and exposure throughout.
- Forward motion may accelerate between rooms, but each doorway threshold must remain visible long enough to prove the route.
