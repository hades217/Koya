# Apartment 106 terrace concept V1

Status: `CONCEPT_FOR_REVIEW`

Geometry authority: `Koya marketing plan Apartment 106.pdf` (`FLP-106`)

## Sales objective

Make the wraparound private terrace read as a usable Brisbane outdoor room and as the final payoff of the walkthrough, while preserving a clear physical route and a full look-back into Living, Dining and the single-island Kitchen.

## Illustrative loose furnishing

- Two low-profile timber outdoor lounge chairs with neutral weatherproof cushions.
- One small round stone side table.
- One compact four-seat outdoor dining setting positioned away from the sliding threshold.
- Varied loose subtropical planters grouped at corners and the balustrade rather than a repetitive continuous row.
- Minimal soft styling: one neutral throw and a small ceramic tray.

## Geometry locks

- Preserve the plan-proportional wraparound terrace footprint.
- Preserve the same wide glazed opening, black frames, tracks, soffit, slab and balustrade.
- Keep a generous unobstructed path through the sliding threshold and along the terrace.
- Preserve the interior Living/Dining/Kitchen relationships, one island, sofa orientation and TV wall.
- Do not add fixed planters, screens, pergolas, outdoor kitchens, pools, fireplaces, steps, extra doors or an unsupported skyline.

## Evidence status

Furniture, planting and outlook are illustrative concept design only. Exact terrace dimensions, finishes, landscape specification, furniture inclusion and view remain unavailable from the marketing plan.

## Video role

If approved, this image becomes the visual target for the final reference-video beat: camera crosses the threshold, settles on the outdoor living arrangement, then turns back to this buyer-facing composition. It is not itself proof of a continuous path.
