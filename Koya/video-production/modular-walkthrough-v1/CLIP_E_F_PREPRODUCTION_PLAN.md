## Scope

The active preparation order is:

1. Clip E - Living hub to compact MPR.
2. Clip F - Living hub to private Terrace, used as the final spatial payoff.

Both clips use the `modular_branch_walkthrough` contract. Each branch starts from its locked Living hub orientation, moves physically forward through the real opening and ends at the destination. ## Shared camera standard

- 16:9 landscape, target 1920x1080.
- First-person eye height approximately 1.58 m.
- Brisk but recognisable human walking, not floating or a static-image zoom.
- Rectilinear 32-35 mm-equivalent public-space perspective.
- Neutral Brisbane daytime throughout.
- No person, hands, operator reflection, text, logo or watermark.
- One fixed scene and one continuous deterministic reference video per clip.
- Default Seedance input for topology-sensitive V2V: that reference video alone.

## Clip E summary

Route: locked west-facing Living/Dining hub -> travel west beside the Dining zone -> approach the MPR's only northeast-corner door -> cross between the same jambs -> reveal the compact MPR -> stop inside.

Buyer objective: understand that the MPR is a small flexible study/reading/guest-support room, not a third full-size bedroom.

Target duration: 5 seconds, subject to normal-speed deterministic-preview proof.

Required structural checkpoints:

1. Hub orientation with Dining and the solid MPR volume readable.
2. Forward approach along the public circulation path; no shortcut through the MPR wall.
3. The single northeast MPR door becomes fully identifiable.
4. Pre-threshold frame with both jambs visible.
5. Physical threshold crossing with jamb parallax.
6. Fully inside before the reveal turn.
7. Compact room-width reveal with desk and small daybed/bench treatment.
8. Stable final frame retaining a doorway or public-room continuity cue.

## Clip F summary

Route: locked east-facing Living hub -> approach the open Living/Terrace glazing -> cross the same sliding-door track -> move onto the private Level 1 Terrace -> turn along the wraparound outdoor edge -> stop with both terrace depth and the Living connection readable.

Buyer objective: show the genuine indoor/outdoor relationship and make the terrace the closing spatial payoff.

Target duration: 5 seconds, subject to normal-speed deterministic-preview proof.

Required structural checkpoints:

1. Hub orientation with Living and the real terrace threshold visible.
2. Forward approach with the same glazing mullions and track persisting.
3. Pre-threshold frame with the opening edges visible.
4. Physical crossing over the sliding-door track.
5. Fully outside before the camera turns.
6. Reveal the wraparound terrace edge at Level 1.
7. Stable endpoint with Living still visible behind or camera-left.
