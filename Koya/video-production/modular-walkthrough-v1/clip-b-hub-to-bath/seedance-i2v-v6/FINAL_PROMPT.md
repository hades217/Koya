## Inputs

- No intermediate reference images.

- Resolution: 720p.

## Production prompt

Create a literal five-second single-take first-person property walkthrough in one continuous physical apartment. The first supplied image is the exact first frame and the second supplied image is the exact last frame. There is only one camera, one continuous timeline and one fixed architectural world.

0.0-2.5 seconds: immediately dolly quickly and smoothly west through the real clear aisle. The single kitchen island remains on camera-left and slides behind with strong natural parallax. The fixed kitchen cabinet run remains on camera-right and also passes continuously. After the island ends, the solid MPR wall becomes the left boundary. The west stop and Bath turn approach rapidly; this is a short apartment distance, not a long corridor.

2.5-3.3 seconds: without stopping, perform one smooth continuous 90-degree clockwise right turn toward the single real main-Bath doorway. Walls, cabinetry, floorboards and the doorway remain rigid and maintain normal perspective throughout the turn.

3.3-4.3 seconds: continue forward through the existing open doorway. The two jambs pass naturally along the frame edges. The open door remains hinged at the left/west jamb and folded inside against the west bathroom wall. Timber flooring ends at the fixed threshold and bathroom tile begins. The camera must pass through the empty doorway opening, never through a wall or fixture.

4.3-5.0 seconds: continue a short distance into the compact bathroom and settle on the exact supplied last-frame composition: vanity left, one toilet centre-right and one compact glass walk-in shower right. Motion continues until the final moment; do not freeze early.

Hard physical constraints: one uninterrupted shot; zero cuts; zero dissolves; zero fades; zero crossfades; zero portals; zero teleportation; zero spatial morphing; zero object replacement; zero static-image zoom; zero pause before the turn. Every frame must be a physically adjacent camera position in the same rigid 3D space. Preserve the Koya pale-oak cabinetry, off-white walls, light stone, natural daylight, compact scale and photorealistic property-film appearance. Eye height 1.6 metres, 28-32mm full-frame lens, level verticals, purposeful stabilized walking speed with subtle natural footfall motion.

Never show Laundry, paired wet-area doors, a bathtub, duplicate fixtures, people, an opening in the MPR wall, an enlarged bathroom, text, logos or captions. Generate only synchronized natural interior ambience: quick soft footsteps changing from timber to tile and faint room tone; no music and no voice.

## Acceptance gate

- Camera motion is continuous for all five seconds.
- Island and fixed cabinets move by real parallax, not scaling.
- The right turn occurs through perspective rotation, not a transition.
- Door jambs persist while the camera crosses the opening.
- No wall, cabinet, fixture or doorway changes identity or position.
- Final frame is reached only by physical forward movement.
