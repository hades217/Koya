## Intent

Create a deterministic 30-second 3D white-model camera rehearsal. It is not the
final sales film. Its sole job is to prove that one camera can fly from the
Brisbane/Toowong world to the same Koya building, acquire one locked resident,
cross every threshold, stay inside one lift cabin, reach Level 1 Apartment 106
and enter the living zone with no cuts or spatial substitutions.

## Assets

- Official Koya facade and location imagery remain the final appearance authority.
- Official Apartment 106 Level 1 plan controls the final apartment adjacency.
- `CHAR-RESIDENT-004 v2` controls the later Seedance resident appearance.

## Customizations

- One deterministic Three.js world and camera path, sampled directly from time.
- One simple resident proxy with locked bottle handedness.

## Notes

- No still-image zooms, dissolves, scene cards, hidden cuts or architecture swaps.
- Elevator rises only to Level 1 because Apartment 106 is labelled Level 1.
