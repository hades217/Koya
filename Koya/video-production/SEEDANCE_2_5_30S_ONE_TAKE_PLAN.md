## Director's correction

This is one uninterrupted moving camera. There are no separate 5-second or 10-second clips, no editorial cut, dissolve, black frame, map card, insert close-up or second camera. Speed changes, pitch changes and real foreground occlusion are allowed, but the camera must retain one forward trajectory.

The camera does not stare at the resident for the whole film. It first sells location and the exact building, then acquires the resident and moves faster than ordinary walking so the complete route fits inside 30 seconds.

- finish outside the apartment door.
