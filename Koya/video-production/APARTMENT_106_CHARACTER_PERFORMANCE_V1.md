## Dramatic intention

She is a real resident returning after exercise and informally showing a guest around her home. She is familiar with the apartment, slightly winded at first and progressively more relaxed. She never behaves like a showroom presenter, game avatar or fashion model.

The emotional progression is:

`arrive and settle -> check the guest is following -> quietly reveal private rooms -> become more relaxed -> welcome the guest into the shared living space -> enjoy the terrace`

## Locked continuity

- Same face, body proportions and deep-brown ponytail as `CHAR-RESIDENT-004` v2.
- Sage-grey long-sleeve athletic top, matte opaque black full-length leggings, white-grey runners, taupe running belt and black sports watch.
- Stainless bottle remains in the LEFT hand throughout.
- The RIGHT hand opens doors and performs the single invitation.
- No wardrobe, hairstyle, makeup, age, face or hand-prop changes.

## Time-coded performance

### 21-24s - Becoming at home

- One visible natural breath after entering; shoulders settle by a few centimetres.
- Fingers briefly adjust around the bottle rather than holding it rigidly.
- Gaze follows the hall, then flicks toward the private-room side; no camera smile.

### 24-29s - Main bathroom and laundry

- Open each adjacent door with the same right hand while keeping her torso in the hall.
- A small shift of gaze from laundry to bathroom establishes the relationship.
- Do not mime washing, point at fixtures or perform sales-presenter hand movements.

### 29-35s - Bedroom 2

- Open the real door with the right hand and transfer body weight onto the forward foot.
- Step half a pace aside so the guest can see the room.
- Look into the room with familiar recognition, then glance over the shoulder only long enough to confirm the guest is following.
- Exit through the same door without posing beside the bed.

### 35-39s - Returning to the kitchen axis

- Exit Bedroom 2 through the same door and orient her shoulders toward the brighter kitchen axis.
- One brief rear three-quarter check confirms that the guest has cleared the bedroom doorway.

### 39-44s - MPR

- Smaller, quicker reveal than a bedroom.
- A slight eyebrow lift and quiet half-nod communicate flexible use; no broad gesture.
- Let the camera inspect while she waits naturally at the threshold.

### 44-49s - Kitchen

- Walking rhythm loosens as the apartment opens up.
- Right fingertips may briefly meet the island edge once, with visible contact and normal friction; never clip through the stone.
- She looks toward the terrace daylight rather than staring at the camera.

### 49-55s - Bedroom 1

- Pace softens. She opens the door, takes two steps in and lets her eyes move from the bed toward the exterior light.
- One small relaxed exhale. Expression is calm and personal, not promotional.
- She turns her shoulders toward the real WIR opening to establish the next route.

### 55-61s - WIR and ensuite

- She walks through the WIR at normal human clearance, avoiding cabinetry.
- At the ensuite threshold she turns slightly side-on, allowing the camera to see past her.
- No mirror-facing pose and no duplicated reflection.

### 61-66s - Returning to the public zone

- Return through Bedroom 1 and the same bedroom door.
- Pace becomes a little quicker and more confident.
- One fleeting asymmetric half-smile when she glances back and sees the guest catching up; less than one second.

### 66-71s - The invitation

- Move into the left third of frame and take one small backward step to clear the route.
- Turn approximately 35 degrees toward the guest.
- Establish warm direct eye contact for about 0.8-1.2 seconds.
- A restrained, genuine half-smile develops rather than appearing instantly.
- Open the empty RIGHT palm toward the living room in one continuous motion; keep the bottle low in the LEFT hand.
- No wave, pointing finger, presenter flourish or exaggerated grin.

### 71-78s - Accepted welcome and terrace

- As the camera passes, her eyes and head follow it naturally; her body does not spin mechanically.
- She falls one or two steps behind instead of teleporting ahead.
- At the terrace, breathing settles and she looks outward first.
- End with a tiny glance back toward the guest and a relaxed expression, while the camera continues moving gently.

## Human detail layer

- Preserve small gait variation, heel-to-toe foot placement and weight transfer.
- Allow restrained bottle swing with inertia; it must not be glued to the hand.
- Retain subtle breathing in shoulders and upper chest.
- Permit a few ponytail flyaways and natural ponytail lag on turns.
- Use realistic blinking at irregular intervals, never synchronized with room changes.
- Keep facial movement asymmetrical and small; real skin texture must remain visible.
- Footsteps, clothing movement and room acoustics change with each surface and space.

## Anti-AI rejection conditions

- Character stares into the lens for more than the invitation beat.
- Identical smile is repeated in multiple rooms.
- Expression snaps between neutral and smiling without transition.
- Feet slide, body floats, hand passes through doors, island or cabinetry.
- Bottle changes hand, duplicates, bends or disappears.
- Ponytail freezes during movement or changes length.
- Skin becomes plastic, facial symmetry becomes artificial or identity drifts.
- Movement resembles a game walk cycle, runway walk or showroom presenter.
- Character teleports ahead after the camera passes her.
- Speaking or visible lip-sync is introduced. The invitation is performed through eye contact and body language.
